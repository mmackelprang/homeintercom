# Housecall — brand guide

**Proposed display name:** Housecall
**Tagline:** *Push to talk, house-wide.*

## Why this name

**Housecall** — a call through the house, on wall units. Alternate: *HomeIntercom* (exact, flat).

## The mark

A wall plate: speaker grille above, one big press-to-talk button below. The whole UX is that button.
